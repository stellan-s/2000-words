# Vocabulary content and licenses

The bundled Spanish vocabulary is generated from:

- [`doozan/spanish_data`](https://github.com/doozan/spanish_data)
- [`doozan/6001_Spanish`](https://github.com/doozan/6001_Spanish)
- [English Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org)
- [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org)

Definitions derived from Wiktionary are distributed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and the
applicable Wiktionary terms. Frequency data is attributed to
`github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords` under CC BY-SA 3.0. Example sentences
are attributed per entry to their Tatoeba contributors under CC BY 2.0 FR.

The machine-generated dataset includes per-sentence attribution in
`assets/data/spanish-2000.json`. It must receive human linguistic review
before a public production release.

FSRS scheduling is provided by
[`ts-fsrs`](https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/ts-fsrs) under the MIT
license.
